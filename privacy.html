Privacy policy

Updated 2026-11-13.

On this iPhone

Practice Rewind records video and sound to help you review your practice. Recording and sound analysis happen on your device. There is no account, advertising, tracking, analytics, or app-operated upload service.

Recordings you keep

Ordinary recordings are temporary. A tag keeps a separate copy of the original recording and its selected playback range for later review. You may add optional labels and save a named, dated compilation to the local Library. Compilations keep independent copies and playback ranges until you delete them or delete the app. Tagged takes remain until you clear Review or delete the app. Clearing Review removes tagged copies, not saved compilations or the current temporary recording. If a file cannot be removed, the app reports pending cleanup and lets you retry.

Microphone and voice

Camera and microphone access are needed to record. Optional voice commands use Apple’s on-device English speech recognition. Recognition is off during replay, and transcripts are not saved or sent to an app server. You can turn voice off in Settings or revoke permissions in iPhone Settings.

Device backups

Tagged recordings, saved compilations, names, labels and preferences may be included in backups managed by iOS, depending on your device settings. Practice Rewind does not operate its own cloud backup or synchronization. Manage device backups in iPhone Settings.

Your choices

You can use replay without enabling voice commands. Pause or leave the app to stop playback; leaving interrupts recording. Use Clear all in Review to remove tagged takes. Delete saved compilations separately in Library. Old recordings saved by earlier versions are left untouched; uninstalling the app removes its local data.

Support and external pages

Opening a support or privacy website uses your browser. Its hosting provider may process normal website request information. If you choose to contact support, the information you send is used to respond to your request. Do not include private recordings unless you intend to share them.

Contact

Email support@smason.dev. Include your app version and a description of the issue. Please do not send private recordings unless you intend to share them.

This website

This static site has no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes website requests under its privacy statement. That is separate from recordings processed by the app.
