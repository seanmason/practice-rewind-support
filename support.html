Support

Email support@smason.dev. Include your app version and a description of the issue. Please do not send private recordings unless you intend to share them.

Record and listen

Tap Record, play your passage, then Listen back. Say “Ori, replay” if you enabled voice. Replay repeats until paused. Use the flag to adjust Start or End, and Record to begin again. The original recording remains intact.

Speed and pitch

Tap the speed value below the waveform. Normal speed, 1×, is at the center; Reset restores it. Move the slider and turn Keep pitch on to preserve note pitches while slowing or speeding playback. Pinch the replay video to zoom, drag while zoomed, and use the reset-view button to fit the whole frame again.

Review later

Tap the bookmark during replay to keep the recording and its selected loop. Open Review to play tagged takes in order, skip between them, or scrub. Labels are optional: use the pencil to name a take, or leave it unlabeled. Use Save to keep a named, dated compilation in Library. A compilation plays its takes in sequence and keeps independent copies of the original recordings and chosen ranges. Clear all removes current tags while preserving saved compilations and your current recording. Delete a saved compilation separately from Library.

Camera and storage

Switch cameras before recording or while listening, ready for the next take. Pinch the live preview to zoom. Camera flipping is unavailable during active recording. Position the phone before starting; capture detects its orientation, including when screen rotation is locked. In Settings, choose Compact, Balanced or Detailed video. These change video bitrate while keeping the audio capture path unchanged.

Voice and AirPods

Voice needs on-device English recognition and permission in iPhone Settings. Camera Remote must be enabled in your compatible AirPods settings. Buttons remain available without either feature. Keep the app in the foreground.

Deleting data

Use Clear all in Review for current tagged takes, and delete compilations separately in Library. Uninstalling removes local app data. Manage any iOS backups separately in iPhone Settings.
